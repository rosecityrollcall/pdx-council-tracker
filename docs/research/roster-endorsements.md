## 4. 2026 election context

- **Six seats up Nov 3, 2026:** all of **District 3** (Novick, Morillo, Koyama Lane) and **District 4** (Clark, Zimmerman, Green), who drew the initial two-year terms; winners get four-year terms. All six incumbents are running for re-election ([Wikipedia 2026 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Portland,_Oregon_City_Council_election), [Wikipedia D3 page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland%27s_3rd_City_Council_district_(Oregon))). Districts 1â€“2, mayor, and auditor are next up in 2028.
- **Notable challengers:** D4 â€” Portland police officer Eli Arnold (WW's #3 and Chamber-ranked in 2024) and Jeremy Beausoleil Smith; D3 â€” Michael Johnson, Tyler Frederick, Nicole Tignor Zimmerman; one D3 challenger disqualified over residency ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Portland,_Oregon_City_Council_election), [WW, July 2026](https://www.wweek.com/news/city/2026/07/20/city-council-challenger-drops-out-of-race-after-learning-he-hasnt-lived-long-enough-in-his-district/)).
- **Fundraising (public matching, as of June 2026):** Koyama Lane ~$168k unlocked, Morillo ~$113k, Novick ~$50k ([WW, June 21, 2026](https://www.wweek.com/news/city/2026/06/21/heres-how-much-city-councilors-up-for-reelection-have-raised-so-far-through-city-program/)).
- **2026 endorsements in progress:** United for Portland says council endorsements are coming "later in 2026" ([unitedforportland.org/endorsements](https://unitedforportland.org/endorsements/)); Oregon WFP has begun 2026 statewide rounds ([WFP, March 2026](https://workingfamilies.org/2026/03/oregon-working-families-party-announces-second-round-of-statewide-endorsements/)).
- **No sitting councilor found to be running for another office in 2026** (searches for county chair/governor/legislature bids returned nothing; all six up-for-election incumbents are seeking re-election to their own seats).
